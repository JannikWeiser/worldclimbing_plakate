Event Poster

Create an A3 event poster: add a photo, an info text, a ticket link (shown as QR code) and partner logos. Everything is processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded or stored anywhere.

Upload a photo to get started

Tip: drag the photo with your mouse (on mobile: with two fingers) to adjust the crop. With one finger you can keep scrolling the page normally.

Partner logos (optional)

Select all logos of a group at once. Choosing files again replaces the previous selection of that group. The first logo of "Organised main partners" and "Organiser" sits next to the divider line on the right; further logos follow to the left.

⚠️ Downloading doesn't work in Instagram's in-app browser. Tap ⋯ in the top right and choose "Open in Browser".

Export: A3 print size (3508 × 4961 px, 300 dpi). On devices with a lower canvas limit a smaller size is used automatically.

🔒 Everything runs locally in your browser. No images or texts are ever sent to or stored on a server.
